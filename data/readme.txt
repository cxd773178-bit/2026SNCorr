Data Files

Codebook_post_final.docx: Variable descriptions, coding schemes, and measurement details for the 46 variables in post_final.Rdata, including behavioral decisions, demographic characteristics, and network centrality measures.

Part_I.docx: Bilingual (Chinese and English) questionnaire materials for Part I, containing six peer-nomination questions used to construct the social networks.

Part_II.docx: Bilingual (Chinese and English) questionnaire materials for Part II, covering hypothetical decision-making tasks and demographic questions.

data_agg contains the anonymized data used in the analyses.

post_final.Rdata: participant-level behavioral and network-measure data for 113 participants. Participants are identified by anonymized node_id values.

SNmF_igraph.Rdata: six social network graphs, each containing 127 nodes.

SNmF_all_agg.Rdata: adjacency matrices for the six social networks.
